1About these terms

These terms describe how MattStories Studio is used. The App is owned and operated by Mateusz Nagler, the person behind the “Matt & Stories” channel.

They are published so that YouTube (Google), Instagram (Meta) and TikTok can review how the App uses their APIs.

2The App

  • The App is a local planner for short videos (YouTube Shorts, Instagram Reels, TikTok). It runs on the Owner’s computer in Poland and is reachable only from that computer (127.0.0.1).
  • It lets the Owner plan publications, prepare titles, descriptions and hashtags, and publish videos either manually or, after explicit approval, through each platform’s official API.
  • It has no server, no user accounts, no payments and no advertising.

3Who may use the App

  • Only the Owner uses the App, and only to manage the Owner’s own YouTube, Instagram and TikTok accounts.
  • The App is not distributed, offered to the public or sold. No license to use it is granted to anyone else, and it is not used to manage other people’s accounts.
  • No fees are charged for the App.

4Platform accounts and platform terms

The App connects to the Owner’s accounts with OAuth and uses only official APIs. Use of each platform through the App remains subject to that platform’s own terms:

  • YouTube: YouTube Terms of Service and the YouTube API Services Terms of Service; Google data is handled under the Google Privacy Policy.
  • Instagram: Instagram Terms of Use and Meta Platform Terms.
  • TikTok: TikTok Terms of Service and the TikTok developer terms.

Access can be revoked at any time in the App (Connected accounts) or in each platform’s account settings.

5Approval and publishing

  • Test mode is the default for every platform. In test mode the App only simulates publishing and sends nothing.
  • Live mode must be switched on separately for each platform, and every real publication needs its own approval of the specific video, platform and time.
  • If the computer was off at the approved time, the App does not publish late on its own. It asks the Owner to approve again.
  • The App keeps a history of attempts and does not send the same video to the same platform twice.

6Content

The App publishes only the Owner’s own videos, titles, descriptions and hashtags. The Owner is responsible for that content and for following each platform’s rules, including disclosures such as “made for kids”, altered or synthetic content, AI-generated content and commercial content. The App does not edit or re-encode video files.

7TikTok

Status: the TikTok integration is in preparation. It is waiting for configuration and, if TikTok requires it, verification of the App by TikTok.

  • Once enabled, the App will use TikTok Login Kit and the Content Posting API only for the Owner’s own TikTok account.
  • Before each post the App shows the creator information returned by TikTok. The Owner chooses the privacy level (none is pre-selected) and the interaction and disclosure settings.
  • While the App is unaudited, TikTok allows posts from it only as private (“Only me”), and the App enforces this.

8Platform limits and availability

  • Each platform sets its own limits (for example API quotas, daily publishing limits, video length and format), and these can change.
  • Instagram and TikTok have no scheduling in their APIs, so the App publishes at the approved time only if the computer and the App are running.
  • A platform may reject, delay or remove a post. The App reports the status the platform returns.
